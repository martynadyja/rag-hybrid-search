# Getting Started with CloudSync

## Creating an Account

To create an account, go to cloudsync.example.com/signup and provide your email
address and password. After registration, you will receive a verification email — the
account is fully active only after clicking the verification link.

## Installing the Desktop App

The CloudSync desktop app is available for Windows 10/11, macOS 12+, and major Linux
distributions (Ubuntu, Fedora, Debian). After installation and login, the default sync
folder is created at `~/CloudSync` (Linux/macOS) or `C:\Users\<name>\CloudSync`
(Windows).

## Your First Sync

All files placed in the sync folder are automatically uploaded to the cloud within a
few seconds of being saved. Sync status is shown as an icon next to each file: a green
checkmark means synced, a blue circle means syncing in progress, and a red exclamation
mark means an error occurred.

## Inviting Your Team

An organization administrator can invite new users from the
Settings > Team > Invite User panel. The invitation is sent via email and is valid for
7 days.
