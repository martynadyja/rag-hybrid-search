# CloudSync Pricing

## Free Plan

Cost: $0/month. 5 GB storage limit, up to 3 users, sync across up to 2 devices. No API
access.

## Pro Plan

Cost: $12/month per user. 500 GB storage limit, unlimited devices, full REST API
access, email support with a response time of up to 24 hours.

## Business Plan

Cost: $32/month per user, minimum 5 users per organization. 2 TB storage per user, SSO,
enforced 2FA for administrators, priority support with a response time of up to 4
hours, dedicated account manager for organizations with 20+ users.

## Enterprise Plan

Custom pricing. Unlimited storage, 99.9% uptime SLA, 24/7 support, self-hosting option
in your own infrastructure (on-premise), annual security audit included.

## Billing

All paid plans are billed monthly or annually in advance. Annual billing gives a 15%
discount compared to the monthly price. VAT invoices are issued automatically at the
start of each billing period.
